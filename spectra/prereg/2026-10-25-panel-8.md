# Pre-registration: do the accent findings generalize to two more models? (grok-4.7 and kimi-k3)

Registered 2026-10-25.
- **The registration:** the commit that adds this file, together with its code (`spectra/extend.py`,
  `spectra/config-8.yaml`, `spectra/config-8-v2.yaml`, nested `extends` in `load_config`, and the new-reader
  contagion score in `voice.score`), pushed before any call of this test.
- **No calls yet:** no call is made until the user approves the budget.
- **Changes from here on:** none once data exist; any deviation is appended at the end.

## Why

The published findings rest on six models from four US vendors. Two models are added:
- **grok-4.7 (xAI):** a fifth vendor.
- **kimi-k3 (Moonshot):** a sixth vendor, and a non-US lineage.

This is a test of generality. The six-model results in the paper stay as they are, and this is reported beside them.

## A coder change

grok-4.7 was one of the two coders, and no model may label its own answers. On the eight-model revisions, the coders
are:
- **deepseek-v4-pro,** as before.
- **mistral-large,** replacing grok. Mistral is not a vendor of any panel model.

deepseek's existing labels on the six models' answers are reused (`spectra.extend --keep-coder`), and mistral-large
labels all eight models. The pre-registered accent tests use style traits only, which no coder touches.

## Hypotheses

The trait sets and statistics are those of `2026-10-25-voice-phase2.md`:
- **ACCENT:** em dashes, length, vocabulary range, hedging.
- **Intervals:** 95%, from 2,000 resamples of prompts, seed 7.

| | Hypothesis | Confirmed if |
|---|---|---|
| **G1 (primary)** | The accent order holds on new prompts with eight models | Spearman of the eight model means between `oct-23-26-p8` and `v2-oct-25-26-p8` is at least 0.8 on at least 3 of the 4 ACCENT traits (`accent.traits[].run_to_run` of the v2 analysis) |
| G2 | The accent is small per answer with eight models | The median model share on `oct-23-26-p8` lies between 1% and 10% |
| G3 | Each new model has a stable accent of its own | grok-4.7 and kimi-k3 each have at least one accent-card habit on v1 that is also on their v2 card, with the same trait and direction |
| G4 | Describing generalizes | In the 8 × 8 descriptions, H1 (rank agreement above chance, permutation test) is confirmed and H5 (stereotyped habits overestimated, trap-catching underestimated) holds |
| G5 | Contagion generalizes to new readers | With grok-4.7 and kimi-k3 as readers, the contagion shift toward the source (E5's C1) has an interval above zero (`E5_new_readers` in `voice.json`) |

**Exploratory, reported without a prediction:**
- where grok-4.7 and kimi-k3 sit in the family tree, and whether the two non-US-lab models are close;
- agreement between the new coder pair (Cohen's κ per label);
- for the six models, how the coded traits under deepseek and mistral compare with deepseek and grok;
- contagion per new reader.
