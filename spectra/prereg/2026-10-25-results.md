## Part A. Do models know how they write? Sample 1

All 36 descriptions came back usable at the first attempt, costing $1.04. Scores against oct-23-26 are primary;
those against oct-14-26 are secondary.

| | Hypothesis | Sample 1, oct-23-26 | Sample 1, oct-14-26 | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| H1 | Ranks the panel better than chance | 0.21, p = 0.003 | 0.23, p = 0.002 | Replicated |
| H2 | No self-advantage (within ±0.25 places) | +0.19 (−0.04 to +0.40) | +0.15 (−0.10 to +0.39) | Not replicated |
| H3 | Behavior known better than style | 0.49 vs 0.21, p = 0.016 | 0.53 vs 0.27, p = 0.016 | Replicated |
| H4 | The fable/opus em-dash gap is missed | measured +2.54, seen +0.37 | measured +2.52, seen +0.37 | Replicated |
| H5 | Stereotyped habits overestimated, trap-catching underestimated | hedging ×3.1, caveats ×15.7, warm opening ×12.0, follow-up offer ×3.3; premise ×0.8, errors ×0.8, ambiguity ×0.7 | same direction on all seven | Replicated |

### What H2 shows

The interval for self minus others runs from −0.04 to +0.40 places. A model placing itself better than the others
place it would make the difference negative, so the interval rules out any self-advantage larger than 0.04 places.
It does not rule out a self-disadvantage of up to 0.4 places, so the equivalence test fails. The pre-registered result is therefore that no self-advantage was found,
but "no difference" was not shown either. In sample 0 the difference was −0.01 (−0.17 to +0.14).

### Reported without a prediction

- **Agreement with sample 0.** The mean Spearman between the two samples' estimates is 0.62. Listing the panel and
  the estimates in a different order was enough to move them a good deal. The most stable traits were premise and
  ambiguity (0.87) and structure (0.80); the least stable was hedging (0.33).
- **Lower overall agreement than sample 0.** Rank agreement was 0.21, against 0.31 in sample 0. The exploratory
  value was optimistic.
- **Siblings.** Seen gaps were 0.62 of the measured ones within a vendor and 0.66 across vendors (oct-14-26: 0.49
  and 0.68). That is no general brand stereotype at oct-23-26, and a weak one at oct-14-26.
- **The best guesser describes itself worst.** gemini-4-argon placed itself 2.61 places off, against 1.61 when the
  others placed it. Recognizing other models' writing and describing one's own look like different skills. This
  was not pre-registered and rests on one sample.
